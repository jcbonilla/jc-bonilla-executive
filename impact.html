Element451

SaaS scaling and AI operations

COO & Head of AI, 2024–2026; strategic advisor since October 2026

The operating challenge

An evolving AI product suite required coordinated priorities across product, engineering, commercial teams, and customer operations.

What I led

I led operating transformation during the shift from CRM to an AI suite. I connected business planning with KPI ownership, functional playbooks, and monthly reviews, and established an evaluation and governance lifecycle for the AI agent portfolio.

Contribution and operating result

An operating structure connecting AI initiatives to cross-functional commitments, review standards, and decisions about what to fund, scale, or retire.

Organizational context

The business experienced growth and a transaction during my board and operating tenure. Those are organization-level outcomes involving the CEO, team, investors, and market conditions; they are not presented as results caused solely by my work.

What I bring to your mandate

A technical transformation needs executive ownership across functions. The operating model determines how product ambition becomes daily execution.

VaynerMedia

Global analytics practice growth

Global Chief Analytics Officer, 2021–2024

The operating challenge

A growing analytics and AI practice needed consistent ways to sell, staff, and deliver work across global offices.

What I led

I led practice expansion and established organization design, talent development, and delivery standards. I standardized scoping, pricing, staffing, and capacity planning, and translated client needs into analytics product roadmaps.

Contribution and operating result

A larger global practice supported by shared delivery standards and commercial operating routines. The career record attributes practice leadership and these operating interventions to my role.

Organizational context

Practice growth was a collective outcome of client demand, executive sponsorship, and teams across offices. Financial and headcount figures are omitted from this public account.

What I bring to your mandate

Capability building requires commercial discipline as well as technical talent. Shared standards help a practice grow while managing delivery quality.

DataKind

Social impact analytics delivery

Director, Insights & Analytics, 2015–2018

The operating challenge

A global social impact data science portfolio needed repeatable discovery, project selection, delivery, and governance across a distributed practitioner network.

What I led

I partnered with the CEO on strategy and established intake, discovery, delivery, and governance practices. I introduced engagement models and evaluated project quality, adoption, and outcomes.

Contribution and operating result

A more structured delivery approach supporting a growing portfolio of social impact data science work. My contribution centered on portfolio and delivery leadership.

Organizational context

Project volume and delivery outcomes reflect contributions from staff, volunteer practitioners, partners, and funders. Delivery scale alone does not establish downstream social impact or causal benefit to communities.

What I bring to your mandate

Analytics creates value when project selection, delivery, and adoption work together. Responsible measurement separates work delivered from impact demonstrated.

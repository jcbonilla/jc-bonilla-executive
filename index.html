The 90-day engagement

A defined mandate, with decisions at each stage.

Days 1–30

Diagnose and align

Identify the constraint, establish the baseline, and agree the scope, resources, decision rights, and acceptance criteria.

You receive
Executive diagnosis, transformation charter, scorecard, and implementation plan.

Decision
Approve the mandate and resource commitments.

Days 31–60

Build and activate

Lead workflow redesign and implementation. Test the capability with the people who will use it and document how exceptions are handled.

You receive
A pilot or initial production capability, named owners, training, and operational measurement.

Decision
Assess readiness for broader operational use.

Days 61–90

Adopt and hand off

Address adoption gaps, compare performance with the baseline, and establish the routines needed to sustain the change.

You receive
Performance review, operating documentation, internal ownership, and a scaling roadmap.

Decision
Sustain, expand, or revise based on evidence.

What progress looks like

Measure the work that changes.

Success measures are agreed for your mandate. Examples include cycle time, throughput, quality, account review coverage, active usage, and cost to serve.

Revenue, retention, and margin effects may take longer to establish. The engagement measures operational progress and tracks longer-term business outcomes separately.

What makes the model work

  • An executive sponsor who can resolve tradeoffs.
  • A named internal owner and access to functional and technical resources.
  • Agreed decision rights, data access, and a production approval process.
  • Weekly working sessions and regular executive reviews.

Capacity, scope, fees, and acceptance criteria are agreed before work begins. Corporate decisions remain with the client unless explicitly delegated.

Before we begin

What is included?

Executive prioritization, operating redesign, AI deployment oversight, cross-functional coordination, team enablement, measurement, and handoff within one agreed mandate.

What needs separate scope?

Major software development, data-platform migrations, extensive integrations, licenses, cloud costs, and organization-wide restructuring are scoped separately. Implementation resources must be agreed.

Is a diagnostic required first?

No. We can begin with the 90-day engagement when the problem and sponsorship are clear. A short diagnostic can help when leadership needs to align on the constraint.

What happens after day 90?

We review the evidence and agree whether the internal team takes over, a new mandate is warranted, or ongoing fractional leadership would help. Continuation is separately scoped.
